David Saeb

Molecular simulation, protein dynamics, and enzyme design

I'm a PhD candidate in Chemical Engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Prof. Kayla Sprenger, and an NSF Graduate Research Fellow. Since February 2026 I've also been a visiting student researcher in the lab of Prof. Frances Arnold at Caltech.

My work asks how protein motion shapes protein function. Using molecular dynamics, enhanced sampling, and machine-learned models of protein structure and dynamics, I study how conformational ensembles govern signaling and lipid recognition in neurodegenerative disease, and how those same ensembles can be exploited to engineer better enzymes.

I completed my B.S. in Chemical and Biological Engineering (with a minor in music) and my M.S. at CU Boulder. If you'd like to talk science or collaborate, feel free to reach out.
